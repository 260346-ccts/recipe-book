• All-purpose flour: 200 g (approx. 1 ½ cups)
• Baking powder: 1 sachet or 2 tsp
• Sugar: 1 to 2 tablespoons (or up to 50 g)
• Salt: ½ teaspoon
• Eggs: 2 large eggs
• Milk: 240–250 ml (1 cup)
• Melted butter: 50 g (or ¼ cup), plus extra for the pan
Instructions:
1. Mix dry ingredients: Whisk together the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt in a large bowl.
2. Combine wet ingredients: In a separate bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, and melted butter.
3. Form the batter: Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and stir gently until just combined (lumps are okay; do not overmix).
4. Cook: Heat a lightly buttered nonstick pan over medium-low heat. Pour in batter, flip when bubbles form on top, and cook until golden brown.
